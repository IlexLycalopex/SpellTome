## Session Recap

- Quarry
- The gang find themselves in the quarry full of murdered human guards. 
- The guard captain has a rod; Rezra tries to give the rod to the goblins to set themselves free but actually further stimulates a racial division. 
- Gwithian cuts off the Captain's head and then plays football with it. 
- A fight breaks out. 
- Rezra gets a godly voice and gets the goblins to subside. Confusion ensues. 
- Rezra largely manages to disperse the goblins (presumably towards an undefended town or orphanage?). 
- The gang leaves.

On the road: 
- Gwithian feeds the horse goodberries - which goes well.  They arrive in Waterdeep in 32 hours with no rest.  All is well. 
- Arrive via the East Gate. 

Waterdeep: 
- Morgan  (an alive, not-dead, with a head guard captain) is on the gate and interrogates the team.
- Jason ( a gobby child) arrives at the scene on a trike. Jason wanted to do "business" with the captain.  Sounds shady... 
- Jason then take the gang the long way to The Adventurers Guild. 
- Gimbletrump storms in, then buys everyone a round!
- They discover the House of Thane is in the Royal Ward. 
- Turns out House Lane has a pet giant crocodile - the gang obviously want to check this out (as does Jason). 
- Jason takes the gang to the Royal Ward (via Banking district and a famous gnomish bank, and the market district). 
- The gang explore the Royal Ward. find the houses they are interested in.  Learnign that House Lane are exotic traders. 
- Gwithian breaks in to the grounds, accidentally unlocking all the doors everywhere. 
- Jason, inexplicably follows in (potentially by being tossed over the wall - who knows?)
- Jason proceeds to agitate the giant crocodile, and then gets eaten. 
- The gang leave in search of House Thane in order to hand over the black-pearls.
